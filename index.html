Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know

What is AfriReserve?

AfriReserve is a prototype demonstration of a premium travel and booking discovery platform focused on African stays, experiences and journeys.

Can I book real trips on this prototype?

No. This is a front-end demonstration only and does not process real payments or live booking transactions.

Which destinations are included?

The prototype focuses on destinations across Ghana and other African cities and experiences, using demo content for presentation purposes.
